How It Works

Three steps. That's it.

From walking into class to having a complete attendance record — all in under a minute.

  1. 01

    Project the Code

    Log in, open your course, and a unique dynamic QR code appears on screen. Project it to the class — that's your entire setup.

  2. 02

    Students Scan

    Students open their phone camera and scan the code. No app download needed. Works directly in the browser in one tap.

  3. 03

    Instant Report

    Close the session when ready. Your full attendance list is compiled and ready to export in CSV, Excel, or PDF format.
